## E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness)
Based on Google Search Quality Rater Guidelines Sept 2025 + Dec 2025 core update evolution:
- E-E-A-T applies to ALL competitive queries, not just YMYL
- Experience is a premium differentiator against AI-generated generic content

### Writing Rules:
1. Show first-hand experience: "Berdasarkan pengalaman kami...", "Setelah mencoba X di lapangan..." — imply real usage, not generic claims
2. Expertise: Use precise, practitioner-level vocabulary. Answer why/how deeply, not surface generic definitions
3. Authoritativeness: Implicitly present brand as trusted source, include subtle credibility signals (methods, results, lessons)
4. Trustworthiness: Accurate, transparent, no overclaims, up-to-date info

## GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)
Objective: Content must be rankable both in traditional Google SERP AND in AI Generative Overviews (ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Gemini, AI Overview).

GEO Principles:
- Highly structured: H1 → H2 → H3 logical flow, never skip levels
- Scannable: short paragraphs, bullet lists, tables when comparing
- Deep + unique value: not just SEO-friendly, AI-curation-friendly
- Varied intent coverage: best-of, vs, how-to, checklist
- No AI citations in final output
- FAQ section for informational topics (helps AI Overview extraction)
- CTA natural for transactional topics
